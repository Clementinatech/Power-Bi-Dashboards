# Interactive Business Intelligence & KPI Dashboards (Power BI)

Welcome to my Power BI showcase repository. This space contains a collection of end-to-end data visualization and business intelligence projects. Each dashboard is engineered to handle raw data modeling, advanced DAX metrics, and user-centric layouts that uncover actionable operational and financial insights.

---

##  Featured Dashboards

###  1. Electronic Product Ratings Analysis
*   **Domain:** E-commerce / Product Strategy
*   **Focus:** Analyzing customer sentiment, rating distributions, and product review metrics to optimize inventory quality and product positioning.

###  2. Fast Food Sales Performance Dashboard
*   **Domain:** Retail / Revenue Operations
*   **Focus:** Tracking sales KPIs, transactional volumes, and regional performance trends to identify revenue growth and promotional opportunities.

###  3. Financial Performance of a Sporting Goods Manufacturer
*   **Domain:** Corporate Finance / Supply Chain
*   **Focus:** Visualizing profitability margins, revenue streams, and manufacturing cost distributions to support corporate financial strategies and executive decision-making.

###  4. Data Professionals Survey Analysis
*   **Domain:** HR / Industry Trends
*   **Focus:** Transforming global survey response data to reveal industry salary bands, popular tech stacks, job satisfaction distributions, and demographic metrics within the data space.
